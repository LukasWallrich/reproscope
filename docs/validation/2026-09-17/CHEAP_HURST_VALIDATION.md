## Pipeline changes exercised

- Source claims have stable physical identities and exact assignment coverage. Chunked contracts and source-bound operational conditions prevent dense-page omissions and destructive masking of method thresholds.
- Requested regression terms are independently resolved from the blind source contracts. Every requested quantity must be computed and checked; a correct calculation for another coefficient cannot pass.
- Source-only method completion declares inferential additions before execution. Independent recipes enforce variable roles and deposited participant identifiers; model approval cannot waive those checks.
- The numerical reference distinguishes direct residual permutation, Freedman–Lane coefficient tests, rank-based procedures and explicit interval methods. Stable residual-trim ties preserve participant selection across implementations.
- Bounded source repair preserves unresolved failures after malformed patches. Generic prompts specify accurate tail probabilities and distribution quantiles without providing target values. A payment rejection ends metered retries; an explicit subscription tier can patch an existing program.
