# Research notes

These files are AI-generated research reports and design reviews commissioned while scoping
reproscope (2026-08-31 to 2026-09-02). They have not been reviewed line by line by a human.
Citations were checked against Crossref and arXiv at the time of writing, but claims, numbers
and recommendations (including suggestions to contact other projects) are the reports' own and
should be verified against the sources before being relied on.
